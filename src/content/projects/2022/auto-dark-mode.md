auto-dark-mode is an application that I created to automatically apply the dark mode theme when the sun sets, similar to macOS/iOS. on Windows, you can schedule the built in blue-light filter (night light) to turn on at sunset. however, you can not do the same for the dark theme. My script solves that issue for me.
